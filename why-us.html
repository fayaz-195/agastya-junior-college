Why Agastya?

Expert Leadership

Agastya College is led by Founder-Director Mr. A. Naveen Kumar, a renowned educationist with over 26 years of experience in competitive exam coaching and college education. His visionary leadership ensures the institution continually strives for excellence in providing quality education.

Small Class Sizes for Better Learning

With a class strength of only 45 students per section, Agastya offers personalized attention, fostering deeper interactions between students and teachers. This leads to better learning outcomes, as teachers can focus on individual student needs, providing tailored guidance and support.

Our Results

Frequent Parent-Teacher Meetings

Every two months, PTMs are held to keep parents informed about their child’s performance, fostering open communication and early intervention when needed.

Continuous Assessment and Support

Students benefit from regular weekly exams, error analysis, and monthly performance reviews, which help identify areas for improvement and provide the necessary support to enhance their academic growth.

Dedicated Mentorship

Each section is mentored by a senior faculty member who closely monitors the academic progress of every student. Regular reviews and corrective actions ensure students stay on track and consistently work toward their academic goals.
